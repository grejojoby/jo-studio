# Hushline Studio

## Register

product

## Users

Solo singers and creators recording on desktop Chrome or Edge. They want understandable controls without audio engineering knowledge. Speakers are used for the current workflow, so hearing the microphone live must be an explicit headphone option.

## Product Purpose

Record or import complete vocal takes, choose a take, add optional backing, gently polish the voice, preview, and export locally. Preserve the existing private, account-free workflow described in docs/intent/hushline-studio.md.

## Brand Personality

Professional, simple, calm. A working audio studio with clear controls and readable feedback.

## Anti-references

Fake waveforms, decorative controls that behave unexpectedly, cramped advanced settings, and unexplained engineering values. These are the specific problems identified by the user during the September 2026 redesign.

## Design Principles

- Show actual audio and recording state.
- Make controls behave as they look.
- Describe the audible result before exposing technical values.
- Keep recording, listening, and saving obvious and reliable.
- Preserve dry takes while effects remain adjustable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain the existing requirements for visible labels, keyboard operation, focus indicators, reduced motion, and status/error announcements. Keep labels readable at desktop sizes and adapt the layout instead of shrinking all text on smaller screens.
